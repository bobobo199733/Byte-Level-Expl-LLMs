Write a Python function called sudoku_solver(board) that solves a standard 9x9 Sudoku puzzle.

Requirements:
- Empty cells are represented by 0.
- Modify the board in-place.
- Use backtracking.
- Follow Sudoku rules for rows, columns, and 3x3 boxes.
- Do not use external libraries.
- Raise a ValueError if the puzzle is invalid or unsolvable.
